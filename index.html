Business Licensing

Licensed, registered
and ready to work.

We handle the filings so you can focus on your business.

Stellar Business Development Agency prepares and files the registrations a Nevada business needs to operate, then helps you stay current every year.

  • New Nevada State Business LicenseWe file your State Business License with the Nevada Secretary of State, together with your formation filings when you're starting a new business.
  • Nevada State Business License RenewalYour Annual List and State Business License renewal filed on time each year, so your business stays in good standing.
  • DBA FilingRegister the name your business operates under (a fictitious firm name) with the county clerk.
  • City or County Business License FilingYour local business license filed with the city or county where you operate.
  • IRS Tax ID (EIN) FilingWe apply for your Employer Identification Number with the IRS for banking, hiring and taxes. The IRS issues EINs at no charge.
